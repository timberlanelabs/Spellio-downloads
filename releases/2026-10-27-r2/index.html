Type it how it sounds. Find the word, then tap to hear it.

Install Spellio

Version 0.1.0 · Build 1 · iOS / iPadOS 18 or later

Install on your iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari on the device registered for Spellio.
  2. Tap Install Spellio and confirm the installation.
  3. Return to your Home Screen and open Spellio.

This private build is for registered devices. If installation doesn’t finish, ask the person who shared this page to check that your device is included.

Once installed, Spellio finds and speaks words on your device. No accounts, ads, or saved typing history.
